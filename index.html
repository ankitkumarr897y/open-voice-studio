Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 • OpenVoice Studio — https://open-voice-studio.vercel.app/

1. No account, no server storage

OpenVoice Studio does not require an account or sign-up. Your reference audio, cloned voice profiles and generation history are stored only in your own browser (IndexedDB / localStorage). We do not run a paid cloud database and we do not upload your voice to our own server.

2. Third-party voice services

When you choose Engine B (Chatterbox / F5-TTS), your text and reference audio are sent directly from your browser to the free Hugging Face Space you selected, so that Space can generate the audio. That service is governed by Hugging Face's privacy policy. If you add your own free Hugging Face token in Settings, it is stored only in your browser and sent only to Hugging Face as a Bearer token.

3. In-browser speech

Engine A in the static build uses your device's own speech engine. No audio leaves your device for that mode.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use only local storage in your browser for settings, voice profiles and history. See our Cookie Policy. We do not sell personal data.

5. Your rights

You can delete any cloned voice or history item from the Studio at any time, and clear your browser storage to remove everything. Export a voice as a .ovoice file before deleting it if you want a backup.

6. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13. Do not clone a child's voice.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the Last updated date above will change.
