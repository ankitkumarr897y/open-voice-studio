Cookie Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 • OpenVoice Studio — https://open-voice-studio.vercel.app/

1. What we use

OpenVoice Studio uses browser local storage (not tracking cookies) to remember your settings, your Hugging Face token and Space URL, your cloned voice profiles and your generation history — all on your own device.

2. Third-party cookies

Hugging Face Spaces may set their own cookies when you use Engine B. Your browser's own speech engine does not set cookies for us.

3. Your control

You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings at any time. Clearing them will delete your saved voices and history in this browser, so export any .ovoice file first if you want to keep it.
